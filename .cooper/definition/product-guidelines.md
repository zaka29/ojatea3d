# Product guidelines

## Voice and visual direction

Use quiet, concise, concrete instructions. Prefer tea-room language and a restrained palette of muted green, clay, wood, warm light, and soft ivory. Keep technical implementation details out of visitor guidance.

## Interaction

Make the active action and its progress clear. Maintain equivalent mouse and keyboard ceremony paths. Preserve native focus and button behavior, and stop held actions when focus or visibility is lost.

Reduced motion changes presentation without skipping ceremony steps. Keep narrow-layout instructions and controls legible. Do not claim complete touch or device support from responsive screenshots alone.

## Assets and performance

Keep geometry and materials replaceable while preserving interaction anchors, receiving openings, home poses, and resting surfaces. Validate replacement assets in the running ceremony before accepting them.

Use local resources for required scene loading. Optional fonts have system fallbacks. Measure representative-device performance before making performance claims.

## Documentation

Use GitHub-flavored Markdown and valid relative links. Distinguish implemented behavior, targets, and unverified future work. Living definitions and requirements stand alone without planning identifiers or positional section references.
